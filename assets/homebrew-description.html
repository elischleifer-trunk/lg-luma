A native OpenGL launcher for LG webOS TVs.

  • Launch apps and inputs, organize favorites, and browse your app library.
  • Upload custom backgrounds, toggle gradient shading, and enable automatic background rotation.
  • HDMI inputs appear as cards at the front of the favorites row.
  • Customize input names and visibility, with live cable and signal status.
  • Search movies and shows from the Search tab and open them in the app that has them (needs a Movie of the Night key), with Rotten Tomatoes scores on each poster (with an MDBList key).
  • Add webhook shortcuts for automations.
  • Show Home Assistant cameras and notifications over whatever is playing.
  • Browse every Home Assistant camera in a full-screen Cameras app.
  • Type into search and other fields from your phone, through a Home Assistant notification.
  • Personalize with greeting, date, clock, and weather.
  • Check for Luma updates and install verified releases from Settings → System.

Available in Dutch, English, French, German, Italian, Polish, Portuguese (Brazil), Russian, Simplified / Traditional Chinese, Spanish, Swedish and Vietnamese.
